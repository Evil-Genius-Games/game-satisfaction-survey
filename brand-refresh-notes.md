# Evil Genius Games Survey Brand Refresh Notes

Date: 2026-06-06

## Official visual references observed

The official Evil Genius Games website uses a dark, cinematic visual language with a black or near-black page foundation, a vivid red promotional/top-bar accent, and a white/red/black logo treatment. The site’s homepage hero emphasizes action-oriented tabletop adventure imagery, strong contrast, and large headline moments.

The official logo image referenced by the live site is:

`https://evilgeniusgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/logo-header-1.webp`

## Design direction for the survey

The survey should keep the fast single-card flow and minimal DOM footprint, but shift away from the generic purple gradient. The refresh should use a lightweight CSS-only treatment: dark cinematic background, red accent system, high-contrast white survey card, official logo placement, sharper card shadow, branded progress bar, stronger buttons, and improved input focus states.

The UI should remain readable, mobile-friendly, and fast. No large hero imagery, webfont dependency, or heavy animation should be introduced.
